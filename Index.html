About Life Unlocked

Life Unlocked is a blind-first, text-based life simulation designed primarily for people who use screen readers, Braille displays, keyboard navigation, and other assistive technology.

Important gameplay information does not depend on graphics. Sighted players are welcome to play too, but the interface intentionally focuses on accessible menus, text, optional audio, and functional controls instead of a graphical world.

Use the tutorial if you want guidance, or skip it and explore Bellmont your own way.
