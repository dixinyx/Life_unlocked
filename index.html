Café Tutorial and Help

Café Basics

Start a Café shift, take an order, prepare it, and serve it before the customer leaves.

Progression

Serving customers earns Café XP. Higher management levels unlock menu items, equipment, capacity, and automatic level rewards.

Bakery and Menu

Locked items cannot be ordered. Some menu items require equipment. Smoothies require a Blender.

Machines

Machines can be purchased and upgraded. The Café can eventually own up to four of each machine type.

Employees

Assigned employees work automatically in their jobs. Experienced employees can eventually become eligible for Manager.

Drive-Through

The Drive-Through is intended as a background feature. An assigned Drive-Through Worker operates it while you play the Café.

Money

Sales go to Café money. Tips go to Personal Money. Wages, business expenses, and owner pay are handled automatically.

Accessibility

On-screen buttons are the main controls. Desktop shortcuts are optional. Important customer messages should take priority over routine background information.

Keyboard Commands and Help

Desktop shortcuts during an active shift: Alt+T Take Order. Alt+P Prepare. Alt+S Serve when no Cashier is assigned. Alt+R Repeat Current Order. Alt+C Café Status. Alt+N New Customer. Alt+L Clean Table. Alt+U Most Urgent Customer.

Café navigation shortcuts: Alt+I Inventory. Alt+M Machines. Alt+B Bakery. Alt+E Employees. Alt+F Finances. Alt+G Statistics and Progress.

On iPhone and other touch devices, use the regular on-screen buttons.
